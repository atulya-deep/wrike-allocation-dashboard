People utilisation

Client hours logged in the Account Management space (within the region/account filter) ÷ working-day capacity (8 h × Mon–Fri days). Internal and non-client spaces aren't included. The current month counts working days up to the refresh date.

How these numbers are built
  • Every night a GitHub Action reads all time logs, projects and budget fields in Wrike's Account Management space through the Wrike API and rebuilds this page.
  • Each time entry is assigned to its PO (or PoV / business-development project) by walking up Wrike's folder hierarchy, and to an account the same way. Where an archived item no longer links to an account, the account is taken from the item or task name; what still can't be placed shows as "Unassigned".
  • Budget €, current budget €, PD, Total PO Hours and avg rate are PO custom fields as entered in Wrike. POs with no hours budget, or one that looks incomplete, are flagged rather than corrected.
  • Account names that differ only in spelling or capitalisation are merged. Nord and Nordix are shown as Nordics. Accounts with no Celonis Region show "Not set".
  • Hours logged on future dates count in totals but not in utilisation.
